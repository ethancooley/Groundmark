Set up Overview Check

Two quick steps. Everything runs on this computer, so there’s no account, no server, and nothing to pay.

  1. 1

    Let it read the pages Google cites

    To check a sentence, Overview Check opens the links Google attached to it, the same as clicking them yourself. It only does this on Google search pages, and it opens pages without your cookies, so sites never see your logged-in accounts.

  2. 2

    Turn on the on-device AI

    Verdicts come from Gemini Nano, the language model built into Chrome. It runs on your computer, so the sentences you check never leave it. Chrome downloads the model once.

You’re set.

Search on Google. When an AI Overview appears, each sentence gets a verdict. Hover a badge to see the evidence.

What the on-device AI needs

Chrome 138 or later on Windows 10 or 11, macOS 13 or later, Linux, or a Chromebook Plus. At least 22 GB of free disk space, and either a GPU with more than 4 GB of memory or 16 GB of RAM with 4 or more CPU cores. If this computer can’t run it, Overview Check still works in lite mode, which matches words and figures instead of reading meaning, and labels every result as a lite check.
